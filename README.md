# 🚗 Road Accident Analysis & Data Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This interactive Excel dashboard analyzes road accident data across key environmental, geographic, and vehicle dimensions. It unifies complex casualty and traffic safety metrics into a single executive dashboard powered by Excel PivotTables, slicers, and dynamic charts to support data-driven decision-making for public safety and urban planning.

## Business Problem Solved
Transport departments and road safety authorities struggle to pinpoint high-risk factors causing severe casualties across different infrastructure settings. This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing:
* **Infrastructure & Road Type Safety Tracking:** Identifying casualty hotspots across road layouts (e.g., single carriageways vs. dual carriageways) to guide infrastructure investments and signage improvements.
* **Vehicle Risk Profile Optimization:** Pinpointing high-impact vehicle categories (such as passenger cars and freight vans) to optimize traffic regulations, driver safety programs, and speed limit enforcement.
* **Environmental & Seasonality Monitoring:** Tracking casualty trends across light conditions, weather types, and road surface states (dry vs. wet) to optimize emergency response readiness and seasonal safety campaigns.

## Key Insights & Metrics
* **Total Casualties:** 417,883 casualties recorded across the dataset.
* **Casualty Severity Distribution:**
  * **Slight:** 84.1% (351,529 casualties)
  * **Serious:** 14.2% (59,313 casualties)
  * **Fatal:** 1.7% (7,041 casualties)
* **Vehicle Type Mix:** Cars represent the vast majority of casualties at **333,485 (79.8%)**, followed by Vans (33,472), Motorcycles (31,618), Buses/Coaches (14,642), Agricultural Vehicles (1,500), and Other types (3,166).
* **Geographic Demographics:** Urban areas registered a larger casualty burden (**258,806 / 61.9%**) compared to Rural areas (**159,077 / 38.1%**).
* **Road Layout Concentration:** Single Carriageway roads account for the largest share of casualties (**308,761 / 73.9%**), far exceeding Dual Carriageways (61,770), Roundabouts (27,477), One-way Streets (7,358), and Slip Roads (5,053).
* **Lighting & Surface Conditions:** Over **305,404 casualties (73.1%)** occurred during daylight, while dry road surfaces represented the major share at **276,437 casualties (66.2%)** compared to wet or icy/snowy conditions.
